Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you operate 24 hours?
+
Yes! We operate 24/7, 365 days a year — including public holidays. Whether you need a pickup at 3 AM or a midnight drop-off, our drivers are ready.
How far in advance should I book?
+
We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance, especially for early morning or late-night trips. Last-minute bookings are accepted subject to availability — just WhatsApp us to check.
What vehicles do you have?
+
We offer sedans (1–4 passengers), MPVs (1–7 passengers), and Hi-Ace vans (up to 13 passengers). All vehicles are well-maintained, air-conditioned, and cleaned before every trip.
Is the price in the estimator final?
+
The estimator gives a rough price range to help you plan. Final confirmed pricing is agreed via WhatsApp before your trip — no hidden charges. Toll and parking fees may apply for some routes.
What is airport paging service?
+
Our driver waits at the Arrival Hall holding a placard with your name or your guest's name. Great for welcoming VIPs, first-time visitors, or anyone unfamiliar with KLIA. Just request this when booking.
Can I bring large luggage or extra bags?
+
Absolutely. For large groups or heavy luggage, we recommend choosing the MPV or Hi-Ace for extra boot space. Mention your luggage details when booking so we can assign the right vehicle.
How do I pay?
+
We accept cash and bank transfer (TNG / DuitNow). Payment is made directly to the driver upon arrival, or via transfer before the trip. All transactions are confirmed via WhatsApp.
